### Code adapted from ItemRack

- `ShowItems/Window.lua`: `UI.Panel`, `UI.SmallButton`, `UI.NumberBox` and `UI.Slider` re-implement in Lua the XML templates `ItemRackOptSubFrameTemplate`, `ItemRackSmallButtonTemplate`, `ItemRackNumberEditBoxTemplate` and `ItemRackOptSliderTemplate` from `ItemRackOptions.xml`. Sizes, offsets, texture coordinates, colors and gradients are the same (for example the 194 x 282 sub frame, the 32 x 20 number box with 12 x 29 border pieces and the 96 x 17 slider with `0.65` gray labels). The backdrop tables in `ShowItems/Menu.lua` and `ShowItems/Window.lua` also repeat the ones in `ItemRack.xml` and `ItemRackOptions.lua`.
- `ShowItems/Sets.lua`: `CollectIcons` and `BuildIcons` follow `ItemRackOpt.PopulateInitialIcons` in `ItemRackOptions.lua`: 20 placeholder icons, then `INV_Banner_02` and `INV_Banner_03`, then the macro icon list obtained with `RefreshPlayerSpellIconInfo`, `GetMacroIcons` and `GetSpellorMacroIconInfo`. `RefreshSlotIcons` follows `ItemRackOpt.PopulateInvIcons`.
- Layout and behavior ideas (the window and tab layout, the Sets and Profiles tabs, the grid menu, the equip queue in combat) follow ItemRack according to comments in the code. For these no verbatim code was found: only a few trivial lines such as `if not InCombatLockdown() then` match.

### What was not found

No verbatim copy of ItemRack functions was found in `Core.lua`, `Trackers.lua`, `Flyout.lua`, `Favorites.lua`, `CharacterMenu.lua`, `ItemTip.lua`, `Options.lua`, `Profiles.lua`, `Minimap.lua` or `Slots.lua`. A line-by-line comparison of all ShowItems Lua files against all ItemRack Lua and XML files found 27 matching lines of 28 or more characters, all of them standard Blizzard backdrop constants or trivial statements.
